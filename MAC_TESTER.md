# Progression Launcher v0.4.4 for macOS

Choose **arm64** for Apple Silicon (M-series) Macs or **x86_64** for Intel Macs from this release. Extract the ZIP and open **Progression Launcher.app**.

These remain test builds. The app is ad-hoc signed, but not Apple-notarized.

Please check path detection, PLAY NOW, pinned local mods, and Optimize Textures. Close RimWorld before optimizing textures. Original PNGs and frozen profiles are preserved.

The build includes the native todds 0.4.1 texture encoder and its license. Report your Mac model, macOS version, and any error from the launcher status panel.
